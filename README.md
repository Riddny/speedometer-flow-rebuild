# Growth Audit — Speedometer GPS

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/speedometer-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to get more revenue from the installs Speedometer GPS: Speed Tracker already gets. There's also a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall, rebuilt in the app's own design. Prepared by Adapty, October 2026.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give the trial a second screen, laid out day by day | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Turn your speed-limit slide into the real setting | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Show their speedometer before the price | CR +10–20% |
| 04 | Make the paywall headline their own setting | CR +15–20% |
| 05 | Put your 4.6 and what Pro unlocks on the paywall | CR +10–15% |

Every screen uses your design: the phone outline, the glowing dial, the red speed-limit sign, the uppercase headlines and the blue button. We changed only the order of the screens, and added a few things the app already has but doesn't show at the right moment.

**Grounding.** Prices, ratings and feature lines come from the app's own in-app screens, its App Store listing and its App Store screenshots. The trial price (₪29.90 a week after 3 free days) is the one shown on the app's own paywall. The 4.6 rating from 8.9K ratings is the US App Store figure.

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Speedometer GPS.*
